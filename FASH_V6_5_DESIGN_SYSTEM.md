# FASH v6.5 Customer UX Refresh

## Design direction
FASH v6.5 moves the customer frontend toward one restrained marketplace system.

### Rules
- No decorative emoji-based interface.
- No unnecessary gradients.
- Cards are used for content grouping, not every section.
- Blue is reserved for interactive emphasis.
- Borders are subtle and shadows are limited.
- Page titles, spacing, forms and empty states share common rules.
- Mobile spacing is deliberate rather than simply shrinking desktop layouts.
