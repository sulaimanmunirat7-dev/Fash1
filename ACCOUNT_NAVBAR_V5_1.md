# FASH v5.1 Navbar and Account Fix

## Greeting behavior
- Guest: **Hello, Guest**
- Existing account login: **Welcome back, Name**
- Newly registered account: **Welcome, Name**

## Account menu
- Account overview
- My orders
- Saved items
- Notifications
- Seller Dashboard (approved sellers only)
- Account settings
- Sign out

## Navbar
This release starts from the last known working v4 navbar and avoids the v5 greeting patch that could break navbar JavaScript.
