# FASH Professional Account Upgrade v4

## Updated account experience
- Reworded the greeting to feel more natural and marketplace-focused.
- Improved labels: My orders, Saved items, Notifications, Saved stores, Account overview and Account settings.
- Added a premium account-dropdown visual treatment.
- Seller Dashboard is intended to remain role-aware and seller-only.
- Ordinary customers should not see seller navigation.

## Recommended account dropdown structure
1. Profile identity and account overview
2. My orders
3. Saved items
4. Notifications
5. Saved stores
6. Account settings
7. Seller Dashboard (approved sellers only)
8. Sign out

The global account CSS is in:
`frontend/FASH/css/account-marketplace-upgrade.css`
