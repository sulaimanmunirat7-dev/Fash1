# FASH Master Changeset
This build fixes the product status migration order, adds category/return/notification persistence,
adds three-step seller registration with store setup captured for post-approval DRAFT creation,
separates seller approval from Verified Seller status, enables draft/hidden product saves without a store
while requiring a store to submit for approval, compresses product images in the browser, improves account
fallback rendering, and fixes category dropdown stacking and seller settings dark-theme persistence.
