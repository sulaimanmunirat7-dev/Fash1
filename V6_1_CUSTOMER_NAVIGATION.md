# FASH v6.1 Customer Navigation

Customer navigation is now the priority before seller work.

## Primary customer navigation
- Home
- Categories
- Search
- Cart
- Account

## Guest account
- Hello, Guest
- Sign in
- Create account

## Logged-in customer account
- Welcome, Name (immediately after signup)
- Welcome back, Name (normal login)
- Account overview
- My orders
- Saved items
- Notifications
- Settings
- Sign out

## Account dropdown design
The dropdown is deliberately compact:
- identity/account header
- four most useful customer shortcuts in a 2x2 grid
- settings + sign out at the bottom
- Seller Dashboard appears only when the authenticated user is an approved seller

## Customer journey priority
1. Home → browse products
2. Search and categories
3. Product details
4. Add to cart
5. Cart
6. Checkout
7. Order confirmation
8. My orders
9. Returns/support
